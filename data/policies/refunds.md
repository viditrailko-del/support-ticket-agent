# Refunds

How much we refund, and until when. Applies to every order unless another policy page says otherwise.

## REF-1 Refund window
Delivered items can be returned for a refund up to and including 30 days after the delivery date.

## REF-2 What a refund covers
A refund covers the price paid for the returned items. The original shipping fee is not refunded, except when an order is cancelled before it ships or is lost in transit.

## REF-3 Refund limit
The total refunded on an order can never be more than the amount paid for that order, including any refunds already issued.

## REF-4 Payment method
Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

## REF-5 Late requests
Refund requests made more than 30 days after delivery are declined, unless the VIP, damaged items or warranty policy applies.
