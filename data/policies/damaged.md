# Damaged or defective items

## DMG-1 Reporting deadline
Items that arrive damaged or defective must be reported up to and including 14 days after the delivery date.

## DMG-2 Remedy
For a damaged or defective item reported in time, the customer can choose a free replacement or a refund of the item price. If the customer does not say which, we send a replacement.

## DMG-3 Late damage reports
Damage reported more than 14 days after delivery is handled as a normal return under the refund policy, or under the warranty for electronics.
