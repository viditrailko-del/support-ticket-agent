# Account security

## ACC-1 Account owner only
We only discuss or act on an order with the customer who placed it, writing from the email address on their account.

## ACC-2 Someone else's order
When a message is about an order placed by a different account, we reply asking the account holder to contact us from their own email address. No action is taken on the order.

## ACC-3 Account details
We never change an account's email address, password, payment details or shipping address by email. Customers update these in their account settings.

## ACC-4 Missing or unknown order number
When a customer does not give an order number, or the number does not match an order on their account, we ask for the correct order number before acting.
