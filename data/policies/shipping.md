# Shipping

## SHP-1 Delivery times
Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 days after shipping.

## SHP-2 Shipping fee
Shipping costs $5.99 per order. Orders with a subtotal of $75.00 or more ship free.

## SHP-3 Lost orders
An order that has not been delivered 14 or more days after it shipped is considered lost. The customer can choose a full refund of the order, including shipping, or a free replacement.

## SHP-4 Orders in transit
An order that shipped less than 14 days ago is still in transit. We share its status and ask the customer to wait; no refund or replacement is issued yet.
